Simple Omelette Recipe

An easy quick dish, perfect for any meal. This clasic omelette combines egg cooked to perfection optionally filled with your choice of cheese, vegetables or meat

Preparation time

  • Total Approximately 10 minutes
  • Preparation 5 minutes
  • Cooking 5 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2-3 large eggs
  • Salt, to taste
  • Pepper to taste
  • 1 tablespoon of butter or oil
  • Optional Fillings cheese, dried vegetables, cooked meat, herbs

Instructions

  1. Beat the eggs:In a bowl with a pirch of salt and pepper until they are well mixed. You can add a tablespoon of water and milk for a fluffer texture.
  2. Heat the pan:Place a non-stick frying pan over medium heat and add butter or oil
  3. Cook the omelette:Once the butter is melted and bubling, pour in the eggs till the pan to ensure the eggs evenly coat the surface
  4. Add fillings(optional): When the eggs begin to set at the edges but they are still slightly rumy in the missle, sprinkle your choosen filling over one half of the omelette.
  5. Fold and serve:As the omelette continues to cook, carefully lift one edge and fold it over the filling . Let's cook for another minute, then side it onto a plate
  6. Enjoy:Serve hot, with additional salt and pepper if nedded.
